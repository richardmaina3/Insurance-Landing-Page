# MainaJP Site — Product Requirements

## 1. Purpose
- **Who is this for?** An independent insurance sales professional specializing in corporate and personal *international health insurance* policies (e.g., Bupa-style global health plans), and the prospective clients (individuals and businesses) they want to reach.
- **What problem does it solve?** Prospective clients currently have no single, shareable place online to learn who this person is, what they offer, and how to get in touch. The site acts as the professional's primary point of contact and credibility signal.
- **What does the product do?** A single-page website that introduces the professional, explains the international health insurance services offered (corporate and personal), builds trust through experience/social proof, and lets visitors reach out via a contact form.

## 2. Goal type & rigor
**Production.** This is a real, live site meant to be shared with actual prospective clients — it should look polished and function reliably, even though the scope (one page) is small.

## 3. User flows

### Flow 1: Prospect learns about services and reaches out
1. Visitor arrives at the site (via shared link, business card QR code, social media, etc.).
2. Visitor sees a hero section identifying the professional and their focus (international health insurance for individuals and businesses).
3. Visitor scrolls (or uses sticky nav) to a Services section describing corporate vs. personal international health policy offerings.
4. Visitor reviews an About/Experience section establishing credibility (background, years in the field, credentials once available).
5. Visitor reviews a social proof section (client testimonials / trust signals).
6. Visitor fills out a contact form (name, email, message/inquiry type) and submits.
7. Submission is emailed to the professional; visitor sees a confirmation message on the page.

### Flow 2: Prospect wants to skip straight to contact
1. Visitor arrives already knowing they want to reach out (e.g., referral).
2. Visitor uses sticky nav "Contact" link to jump straight to the form, or uses a direct phone/email link if provided.
3. Visitor submits the form or contacts directly.

## 4. Phase 1 (MVP) requirements
- One-page responsive site with sticky navigation (Home/Hero, Services, About, Testimonials, Contact).
- Hero section: name, tagline, focus on international health insurance (corporate + personal).
- Services section: corporate international health policies vs. personal international health policies, described as distinct offerings.
- About/Experience section: professional background and years of experience (placeholder copy until real bio/credentials are provided).
- Social proof section: testimonials/client trust signals (placeholder testimonials until real ones are collected).
- Professional photo(s) integrated (already available as an asset).
- Contact form (name, email, message) that emails the submission to the professional; simple success/error feedback on submit.
- Mobile-responsive layout.
- Deployed to a live, shareable URL.

## 5. Explicitly out of scope for Phase 1
- Blog / articles / resources section.
- Multi-language support.
- Client login, quote calculators, or policy-comparison tools.
- CMS or admin panel for editing content without a code change.
- Payment processing or online policy purchase.
- Calendar/booking integration (contact form only for v1).
- Analytics dashboards beyond basic page-view tracking (may add simple analytics later, not required for v1).
- SEO/content marketing strategy beyond basic on-page metadata.
